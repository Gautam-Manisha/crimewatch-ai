CrimeWatch AI Incident Intelligence Knowledge Base

FIGHTING

Definition:
A physical confrontation or violent altercation involving one or more people.

Potential indicators:
- Physical striking
- Pushing or grappling
- Aggressive physical interaction
- Multiple people engaged in a confrontation

Incident handling:
- Record the exact timestamp of the detected event.
- Preserve the original video evidence.
- Review the surrounding footage before and after the detected event.
- If the situation presents an immediate safety risk, escalate according to the applicable security protocol.
- Do not modify the original evidence.

Evidence considerations:
- Preserve the original recording.
- Record the camera/source identifier when available.
- Record the event start and end time.
- Maintain a clear distinction between model prediction and confirmed human assessment.


SHOOTING

Definition:
An event involving the apparent discharge or use of a firearm.

Potential indicators:
- Weapon-like object
- Sudden muzzle-flash-like visual pattern
- Person handling or pointing a firearm
- Sudden crowd reaction or movement

Incident handling:
- Treat a detected shooting event as potentially high risk.
- Preserve the original video.
- Record the precise timestamp.
- Review surrounding footage.
- Escalate according to applicable emergency and security procedures.

Evidence considerations:
- Preserve original footage.
- Record camera/source information.
- Record timestamps.
- Model predictions should be treated as alerts requiring verification, not as definitive legal conclusions.


EXPLOSION

Definition:
A sudden explosive event characterized by a rapid visual change, blast-like event, or large burst.

Potential indicators:
- Sudden bright flash
- Rapid expansion of smoke or debris
- Sudden structural or environmental change
- Crowd reaction following a sudden event

Incident handling:
- Treat a detected explosion as a potentially hazardous event.
- Preserve the original footage.
- Record the timestamp.
- Review footage surrounding the event.
- Escalate according to applicable emergency and safety procedures.


NORMAL

Definition:
Video activity that does not correspond to the suspicious activity categories currently supported by the detection model.

Interpretation:
A Normal prediction does not prove that no crime occurred.
It only means that the current model did not classify the analyzed temporal window as one of its supported suspicious categories.


GENERAL VIDEO EVIDENCE

For every detected incident:
- Preserve the original video.
- Record the source or camera identifier when available.
- Record the event timestamp.
- Record the model's predicted class.
- Record the model confidence.
- Separate automated detection from verified human assessment.
- Avoid presenting model predictions as legally established facts.


MODEL LIMITATIONS

The current CrimeWatch development model supports:
- Normal
- Fighting
- Shooting
- Explosion

The model's confidence score is not the same as model accuracy.

Accuracy should be evaluated using labeled test data.

Predictions from arbitrary live or uploaded footage should be treated as automated alerts that may require human verification.
